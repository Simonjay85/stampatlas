## Editorial workflow

Every entry begins as a **draft brief**. A writer develops an original article from verified sources, adds source URLs and a fact-check note, and submits it for editorial review. Only an editor or administrator can move it to published. Articles that discuss identification, condition, pricing, rights or provenance must use cautious language and link to the corresponding StampAtlas guide or research tool.
